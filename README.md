# Joy's Digital Studio

Build a fully responsive, pixel-perfect, and modern personal portfolio website for a Digital Marketer using React, Tailwind CSS, and Lucide Icons based on the following specifications:

**1. Design System & Theme:**

* **Color Palette:**
* Backgrounds: Light warm cream/beige (`#EBEFE7`), white (`#FFFFFF`), dark forest/sage green (`#4F5E46`).
* Text: Dark charcoal (`#1F2937`) and muted green (`#4F5E46`).


* **Typography:** Clean sans-serif headings with high contrast. Use an elegant accent script font for callouts.
* **Responsiveness:** Ensure mobile-first layout. Grids should automatically stack vertically on screens under `768px` and display full multi-column layouts on desktop.

**2. Section Breakdown:**

* **Header / Navigation:**
* Left: Brand title **JOY CITHELO GUDYANGA** (Bold) | **DIGITAL MARKETER** (Sub-text).
* Right: Nav links: `Home`, `Services`, `Projects`, `Tools`, `Contact`.
* Mobile: Collapsible hamburger menu.


* **Hero Section:**
* **Headline:** "I MARKET BRANDS THAT **GROW.**" (Highlight "GROW" in dark green).
* **Subhead:** "Strategy • Content • Results"
* **Bio snippet:** "I help businesses grow online through smart marketing strategies and engaging content."
* **CTAs:** Primary pill-shaped button "LET'S WORK TOGETHER" (with email icon) + social icon shortcuts (LinkedIn, Instagram, Email).
* **Visual:** Split layout on desktop (text left, framed profile image right). Stacked vertically on mobile.


* **About & Skills (2-Column Grid on Desktop):**
* **Left (Who I Am):** "I am Joy Cithelo Gudyanga, a passionate Digital Marketer who combines creativity and digital insights to connect brands with the right audience and achieve meaningful results."
* **Right (My Skills):** Clean bullet points: Social Media Management, Content Creation, Digital Strategy, Email Marketing, SEO, Brand Strategy.


* **Services Grid ("What I Offer"):**
* Display a 6-card responsive grid (`1-col` on mobile, `2-col` on tablet, `3-col` on desktop).
* Cards should be white with subtle drop-shadows, rounded corners, soft hover-lift effects, and outlined icons for:
1. Social Media Management
2. Content Creation
3. Paid Advertising
4. Email Marketing
5. SEO
6. Brand Strategy




* **Projects Showcase:**
* Responsive 3-card grid featuring project cards with top image containers and bottom title banners:
1. Social Media Campaign
2. Paid Ad Campaign
3. Email Marketing


* Add a right-aligned "VIEW ALL" link at the bottom right.


* **Footer / Contact & Tools:**
* **Tools Row:** Displays icons and titles for: Canva, Meta Business Suite, Google Ads, MailerLite, CapCut, Google Analytics.
